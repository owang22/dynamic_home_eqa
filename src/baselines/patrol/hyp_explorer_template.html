Companion page: Shift Baselines (the follow-on baselines on the same households).

The mixture starts the week with a library of competing weekly models of the home, written from one walkthrough and the residents' intro cards. Each patrol pass re-weights the library; when the evidence goes against a document's claims, the writer is asked to add documents. This page shows which documents carry the belief, how their weights move through the weekend and the event days, and whether being told about a shift changes anything.
