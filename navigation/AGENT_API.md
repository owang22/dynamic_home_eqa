# Driving the Go2 through the console: a guide for agents

The console (`go2_dashboard.py`, `http://172.17.40.64:8080`) runs the dog for everyone. Agents use `/agent/*`
and the read-only GETs. Everything here is taken from the code (`go2_agent.py`, `go2_dashboard.py`,
`go2_mapper.py`, `go2_nav.py`, `go2_markers.py`, `go2_trail.py`, `go2_history.py`). The full machine-readable
list, with every field and constant, is `api_spec.json`, generated by `tools/make_api_spec.py`.

## The rules

- **One owner at a time.** `claim`, or any tool call, takes control and refreshes it. It lapses after **60 s**
  with no call. `status`, `stop`, `release` and `say` need no claim, and `say` does not refresh it.
  Release when you are done.
- **A person always wins.** Driving at the console, *Take over*, or the console's STOP aborts your running tool
  (`stop_reason: person`, or `stop` for STOP) and locks agents out. While locked out, every call that needs a
  claim gets `409 {result: refused, stop_reason: refused}`. The lock ends when the person presses *Hand back*, or after **300 s** with
  no console command. Then poll `status` every 10–30 s and don't fight for control.
- **STOP is always allowed.** `POST /agent/stop` from anyone aborts the running tool and stops the dog. It does
  not change the owner.
- **One tool at a time.** Tools block until the motion ends. A second call while one runs gets
  `409 {result: error, reason: "busy: ...", stop_reason: busy}`.
- **POSTs only from the lab network** (localhost, 172.17.0.0/18). Anything else gets 403.
- Every POST body is JSON and must contain `client` (your name; 400 without it). Add `note` (what you are doing
  and why). The console shows it and keeps it with your photos.

## Frames and signs

| thing | convention |
|---|---|
| `odom` | the dog's own frame. It restarts at every reboot and its heading drifts standing still (median +1.2°/min, from −4 to +6°/min per period; pose logs 10-23 to 10-25). `move` and `turn` work on odometry only |
| `map` | the saved lab map. `map = R(th)·odom + t`, with `T = (tx, ty, th)` from line-up plus continuous drift tracking. Places, `go_to`, `/nav/plan`, `/trail/*` and `relocalize` are all in map metres |
| `yaw` | radians, counter-clockwise from +x, in [-π, π). `*_deg` fields are degrees |
| `turn deg` | **+ = left** (counter-clockwise) |
| `look deg` | + = camera up |
| `move m` | + = forward, − = backward |
| `/markers` `bearing_deg` | **+ = right** of straight ahead (`atan2(x, z)`, camera x points right) |
| `/trail/near` `bearing_deg` | **+ = left** of the frame's centre (map angle to the spot minus the frame's yaw). The opposite sign to `/markers` |

## Every tool reply

`POST /agent/<tool>` returns HTTP 200 with:

- `result`: `done | blocked | no_route | timeout | aborted | refused | error`, plus `reason` when not done
- `stop_reason`: one word to branch on (table below)
- `odom {x, y, yaw}`, `map_pose {x, y, yaw}` at the end (either may be null)
- `fit`: `loc.track_fit` at the end, `lost`: `loc.lost` and `suspect`: `loc.suspect` at the end. `t`: unix time
- `localization_notice`, only when line-up events happened since your previous call:
  `{events, status, fit, runner_up, unsure, summary}`. A jump means map positions you remembered from before it
  may be off.

Bad arguments (missing `m`, not a number, `pose` other than stand/lie, unknown `mode`) give
`400 {result: error, reason: "bad arguments: ...", stop_reason: bad_arguments}`, with no log row. An unknown
`/agent/<call>` is a 404.

| stop_reason | means | usually do |
|---|---|---|
| `arrived` | move, turn or go_to finished | carry on |
| `done` | any other tool finished | carry on |
| `obstacle` | something in the obstacle box (move), or not on the map (go_to) | photo, then go around: turn and move, or go_to again (it avoids that spot for 120 s) |
| `stuck` | no progress for 2 s after one re-plant step: avoidance stopped the dog, or a foot is caught | photo; back up a little or turn; retry. Twice in a row: ask a person |
| `no_route` | the planner found no route | work down the list in *When a go_to fails* below |
| `timeout` | `timeout_s` ran out (default **30 s**) | the dog stopped where it was. Give `go_to` a longer `timeout_s` |
| `stop` | someone's `/agent/stop`, or STOP pressed | check `status` before resuming |
| `person` | a person took over | wait for hand-back; poll `status` |
| `lost` | go_to stopped: `loc.lost` is on | relocalize (below), then go_to again |
| `not_standing` | the dog is lying down (body height < 0.18 m) | `posture {pose: stand}` |
| `fallen` | \|roll\| or \|pitch\| > 0.6 rad | stop everything, ask a person |
| `stale_sensors` | odom or IMU older than 1 s: the dog or driver is off | don't drive; tell a person |
| `refused` | 409: a person has taken over, or another agent has control (any call that needs a claim, including `claim`); or `relocalize set`: that pose fits worse than the current one | wait and poll `status`; for set, send `force: true` only if you are sure |
| `busy` | 409: another tool is still running (often your own previous call after a client timeout) | wait for it, or `stop` |
| `bad_arguments` | 400: missing or wrong arguments (no `m`, not a number, pose not stand/lie, unknown mode) | fix the call; nothing ran |
| `error` | anything else; read `reason` | e.g. `unknown place x`, `no_map_pose`, `no_camera_frame`, posture didn't finish in time |

## Safety checks (all motion tools)

Before moving and every 0.1 s during a move, the tool checks that odom and IMU are under 1 s old, that roll
and pitch are under 0.6 rad, and that body height is at least 0.18 m. Otherwise it stops with
`stale_sensors`, `fallen` or `not_standing`.

- **Ahead:** the dog's own obstacle avoidance (on by default) slows and stops it, and that shows as `stuck`.
  If avoidance is off, a lidar box 0.35–0.90 m ahead and ±0.25 m wide stops it with `obstacle`.
- **Behind:** always checked, against both the lidar and the saved map: a box 0.30–0.75 m behind, ±0.22 m wide.
  Only things at bump height (0.18–0.60 m) count.
- Backing up is slow (0.2 m/s) and short (at most 1 m a call), because the dog sees little behind it.
  Prefer turning around.

## Tools

### claim / release / say / status / stop

- `claim {client}` → `{result: done, lease_s: 60}`, or 409 `refused` with `reason` (a person has taken over,
  or another agent has control).
- `release {client}` → `done`, or `not_owner` if you didn't hold it.
- `say {client, text}` → puts a 300-character note on the console.
- `status {client}` (POST, or `GET /agent/status?client=`) → `owner {kind: agent|human, client, ip, t, idle_s}`
  or null; `current {client, call, args, t, secs}` or null; `safe` (`ok`, or what would refuse);
  `odom`, `map_pose`; `markers` (as `/markers` list); `battery`; `notes`; `log` (last 40 calls); `photos`.
  Call it first and after any surprise.
- `stop {client}` → always `done`.

### move `{m}`

Walks straight `m` metres on odometry: up to 3 m forward, up to 1 m back. Forward speed is 0.25–0.5 m/s
(slower near the end), backward 0.2 m/s. It ends `arrived` within 3 cm. If it stalls (under 3 cm in 2 s), it
does one re-plant step (a short step forward and back) and retries before it gives up with `stuck`.
Returns `moved_m`, plus `side` (front/back) when blocked. It does **not** check `loc.lost`. Typical time is
about 1 s + m/0.5 s.

### turn `{deg}`

Turns in place, + = left, clamped to ±360°. It turns at 0.6 rad/s with a small forward/back rock (so no foot
drags), stops about 6° early for the overshoot, corrects once if it is more than 5° off, and re-plants the feet
at the end. `turned_deg` is what odometry measured, so expect up to 5° error. It ends `stuck` if it turns under
3° in 2 s after one re-plant. Typical time is about 3 s + |deg|/34 s.

### look `{deg}`

Tilts the camera by pitching the body while standing: + = up, clamped to ±30°, 0 = level. Any walk levels it
again. Returns `look_deg` and `body_pitch_deg`. Takes about 1.6 s.

### photo `{inline?}`

Stops, waits until the dog has stood still for 1 s (at most 5 s), and keeps one full-size frame (1280×720).
Returns `photo {id, url, client, t, width, height, still, look_deg, odom, map_pose}`. Fetch the image at
`GET /agent/photos/<id>.jpg` (the last 60 are kept), or pass `inline: true` for `jpeg_b64`. `still: false`
means it never settled; take another if sharpness matters. With no camera frame newer than 2 s, it fails with
`error` (`no_camera_frame`). `GET /frame.jpg` is the live frame without waiting, and may be blurred.

### look_around `{n?}`

Takes `n` photos (1–12, default 4), turning 360/n° after each, so it ends at the start heading. If a turn fails
it returns the photos so far with that turn's result. Allow about n × 9 s, and set `timeout_s` above that
(default 30 s).

### go_to `{place}` or `{x, y, yaw_deg?}`

Drives along a planned route around furniture, then turns to face the place's `target` (or `yaw_deg`, or the
place's own `yaw_deg` when it has no target). It is close enough within 0.20 m.

- Plans on 10 cm cells with 0.28 m clearance; if nothing fits, `tight` 0.20 m; last resort `squeeze` 0.12 m,
  only more than 1.5 m from the dog. On a squeeze route it drives to 1 m before the narrow part, looks with the
  live lidar, and re-plans; if it really is too narrow, it ends `no_route`. Within 1.5 m of the dog a squeeze
  route is allowed only where the dog walked in the last 300 s, i.e. leaving a pocket the way it came in.
- If every clearance fails, one fallback tier ignores the 120 s stall blocks (live obstacles keep full
  clearance). `/nav/plan` and the go_to reply then show `fallback: "live_noblocks"`. Neither
  the fallback nor the trail squeeze is used while `loc.lost` is on.
- A goal on furniture moves to open floor within 1.2 m. A start the map shows as blocked steps out to open floor
  within 0.8 m.
- Within 3 m of the dog the planner uses what the lidar sees now: points 0.18–0.60 m high are obstacles,
  lower ones are open floor. For a cell whose top is 0.6–1.0 m (a desk or table top, a chair back), the
  console looks under the top (since 10-25):
  - anything at 0.18–0.60 m under it (a chair seat, legs, drawers, a box) is an obstacle like any other, and
    the fallback tiers never loosen it. Office chairs used to be invisible here;
  - open floor under a top costs extra (`UNDER_COST` 3 per 10 cm cell, grown by the dog's half width).
- The saved map's solid desk/table-top layer costs the same everywhere (`MID_COST` 3, `MID_FILL` 0.6), also
  beyond the 3 m live range. So routes avoid passing under desks unless every other way is much longer.
  Desk tops are a cost, not a block: blocking them closed every route to marker B on 10-23.
- It re-plans up to 3 times around obstacles that aren't on the map. Each blocking spot is avoided for 120 s.
- It stops with `lost` when `loc.lost` comes on, including when it is already on as the drive starts.
- Returns `moved_m` and `replans`; when `done`, also `route_m` and the place's `target`; `fallback` when any
  plan of the drive needed a fallback tier; `pose_suspect {dx, dy, dyaw_deg, strength, text, marker}` when the
  shift guard was on (see *When the live view looks shifted*). On a failed plan it
  returns `why {saved_map_has_route, cause: "saved map" | "live lidar or temporary blocks", live_new_cells,
  blocks}`; when blocked while driving, `at [x, y]`.
- **Set `timeout_s`**: the default 30 s covers about 10 m. Allow route length / 0.4 m/s plus 5 s per turn.
- Preview first with `GET /nav/plan?place=<id>` (or `?x=&y=`). It plans the same way, without moving.

### posture `{pose: stand|lie}`

Does the same as the console buttons, then waits until the body height shows it (stand ≥ 0.24 m, lie
< 0.18 m; at most 8 s; the thresholds are not yet checked on the dog). Returns `pose` and `body_height`. If the
height never shows it, `result: error` with the reason. `crouch` and `body_height` are not supported (400).
While lying, move/turn/go_to fail with `not_standing`.

### relocalize `{mode}`

Never moves the dog.

- `search` (default): the best line-ups of the recent lidar views within `radius_m` (default 3, clamped
  0.5–10) of `x, y`, or of the current pose.
- `global`: the best line-ups anywhere on the map.
- Both return up to `top` (1–10, default 5) `candidates [{x, y, yaw, yaw_deg, score, fit}]`, best first and at
  least 0.5 m or 20° apart, plus `current {pose, fit, lost, lost_since}`. They apply nothing. A search takes
  about 4–9 s of CPU.
- `set {x, y, yaw_deg | yaw, force?}`: puts the dog exactly there. It is `refused` if that pose's fit is more
  than 0.02 below the current pose's, unless `force`. Success returns `fit_before`, `fit_after` and the new
  `pose` (no `current`), and logs a `loc` event `agent_relocalize`.
- Errors come as `result: error`: `no lidar data yet` (the dog is off or just started), `no current map pose
  to search around; send x, y or mode global`, `stop mapping first`, `a line-up is already running`.

Recipe when lost or unsure: `search` around where you think you are → if the best candidate's `fit` is about
0.75 or more and clearly above the next one, `set` it → check `fit` in the next reply. If search finds nothing
good, try `global`. If nothing fits well, drive a little (turn 90°) so the lidar sees more, then search again.

## Localization numbers

- `loc.track_fit` (and `fit` in every reply): how well the latest lidar view sits on the map's walls. It is
  **about 0.75–0.9 when placed right.** The tracker matches only lidar voxels first seen in the last 20 s
  (`TRACK_FRESH_S`), because `/point_cloud2` is the dog's accumulated voxel map, whose old voxels carry old
  heading drift. After a long time standing still, few voxels are fresh: `track_fit` can dip (or fall back
  to all voxels) without the pose being wrong. Move a little before judging it.
- `loc.lost` comes on when `track_fit` stays **under 0.65 for 10 s while the dog walks at least 0.3 m**. It
  clears at 0.70, or on any new placement or line-up. `move` and `turn` ignore it; `go_to` stops on it.
- `loc.suspect` (with `loc.fresh_fit`, `loc.suspect_since`) is **advisory only and never stops anything**.
  `fresh_fit` is the fit of the voxels placed in the last 10 s, at the current pose. `suspect` comes on when
  it stays under 0.65 for 10 s while the dog walks 0.3 m, and clears at 0.70 or on a placement. It catches
  a bad start much sooner than `lost`, but it can't tell a wrong pose from a changed room. On `suspect`, get a
  `fix_ok` marker sighting or run `relocalize search` before a long go_to. It is in `/state.json` `loc`
  and (`suspect`) in every tool reply, not in `/history`.
- Both the `lost` and `suspect` timers start over after a gap of more than 5 s between judged lidar views
  (for example while the dog stands), so standing still never accumulates time toward them.
- `loc.fit`, `runner_up` and `unsure` describe the *last line-up* only. `unsure` means a score under 0.3, or
  less than 0.05 ahead of the next-best heading.
- `loc.events` kinds: `hand_placement`, `auto_align`, `session_restore`, `new_map`, `drift_correction` (only
  jumps over 0.3 m or 10°), `agent_relocalize`, `marker_disagree`, `unknown` (frame set by hand).
- **Marker checks (logging only, never move the dog):** fix_ok sightings of surveyed markers A–D (≤ 2.5 m, ≤ 45°
  oblique) are pooled over 3 s. Each check is logged in `loc.marker` (last 5: `{t, n, marks, dx, dy, shift, spread,
  action}`, where (dx, dy) is how far the marker says the pose should move). A disagreement of 0.15–1.0 m that
  the lidar doesn't contradict is a `marker_disagree` event (action `would_move`, `lidar_disagrees` or
  `too_far`). At 0.30 m or more it also raises `suspect`. Other actions: `agree`, `spread`.
- `map.mode` in `/state.json`: `localized`, `not_aligned` (no map pose: go_to fails with `no_map_pose`,
  `/nav/plan` gives 409), or `mapping`.

## Not for agents

These belong to people at the console. Don't call them:

- `POST /cmd` (drive channel, takeover, hand back, speed, record)
- `POST /map` (mapping, save, line-up, `localize_at`; use `relocalize`)
- `POST /places`, `/places/move`, `/places/rename`, `/places/delete`
- `POST /map/objects`, `/map/proposals`
- `/label/*` (`/label/detect` also calls the 8B vision model)

## When a go_to fails (`no_route`, `obstacle`, `stuck`)

Check in this order and stop at the first that explains it:

1. **Localization:** `lost`, `suspect` (tool reply) and `loc.fresh_fit` / `loc.track_fit` (`/state.json`). If any is
   bad, the route was planned from a wrong pose: relocalize first, and trust nothing else in the reply.
2. **Planner state:** `why.cause` and `why.blocks`, plus `fallback`. `"live lidar or temporary blocks"` with
   `saved_map_has_route: true` means stall blocks (120 s) or live cells closed it, not the map.
3. **A photo:** chairs, people, bags. Since 10-25 chairs are seen live, so `no_route` in a desk aisle may simply
   be correct. `GET /nav/grid?json=1` shows the narrowest clearance on the last route and what is around it.
4. **Only then** a pose or map problem: relocalize search, `/nav/plan`, ask a person.

Three failures that look alike:

- **Start pocket** (e.g. `door_outside`): the only exit is narrower than 0.20 m within 1.5 m of the dog.
  `why.saved_map_has_route: false`, the dog just drove in, and `/nav/grid` `clear_here_m` < 0.20. It can leave
  the way it came if it walked in within 300 s; otherwise take a photo, then move or turn out a little and retry.
- **Stall blocks**: `why.blocks` > 0 after a `stuck`/`obstacle` nearby in the last 120 s, and the saved map has
  a route. The `live_noblocks` fallback retries without them; or wait 120 s.
- **Lost**: `lost` or `suspect` on, low `fresh_fit`; routes and blocks may sit in the wrong place. Relocalize.

### When the live view looks shifted (`pose_suspect`)

If the live lidar view fits the saved map much better moved by a constant offset (strength ≥ 0.06, ≥ 0.15 m, on
2 checks in a row), the planner **keeps the saved obstacles** instead of letting the live view clear them. Plans
and go_to replies carry `pose_suspect`. The shift is in `/state.json` `live.shift` (`live_shift` on plans):
`{dx, dy, dyaw_deg, strength, text, marker, ...}`, where (dx, dy) is how far the pose (or the map here) seems off.
It means **pose or map**: the guard can't tell which (near B's door the map itself is ~0.3 m off). `marker` pairs
it with a marker check within 10 s, and the text says whether markers agree, disagree or say the pose is right
(then the map is likely off). What to do: stop, take a photo, look for fix_ok markers, run `relocalize search`.
Don't force a route through space the live view would have cleared. On 10-26 all 6 fires were real pose errors.

## Other failure signatures

- **Centring a marker**: `turn(-bearing_deg)`, since a marker's `bearing_deg` is + = right and turn is + = left.
- **`stale_sensors` and `odom: null` in `/state.json`**: the dog or the driver container is off.
- **`turned_deg` a few degrees short with `arrived`**: normal; the tolerance is 5° after one correction.
- **`photo.still: false`**: it never settled within 5 s; take another.
- **A marker with `held: true`, or `fix_ok: false` with `why: [unconfirmed]`**: a one-frame or slightly old
  sighting. Wait a moment and read again before using it as a fix.
- **An expected marker missing**: markers read reliably from the open room, not from under or next to dark
  furniture. Auto-exposure blows out the bright room behind, and marker patches brighter than about 230/255
  are mostly missed. Back out into the open, or turn so the camera isn't looking from a dark spot into a
  bright one.
- **409 `busy`** (`stop_reason: busy`): your previous call is still running (for example after a client timeout). Wait for it, or
  call `stop`.
