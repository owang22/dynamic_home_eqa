# Blind check: two robots' notes, A vs B (written 2026-10-03)

Sources: only the six files in `blind/`. I opened nothing else.

A warning before the answers: in two homes (hh_s2, hh_s32) A writes rules and B writes snapshots. In hh_s48 it is the other way round: A writes snapshots and B writes rules. So the letter does not mean the same writing style in every home. I have kept A and B as the files label them and have not tried to work out which robot is which.

"Snapshot" here means a note that restates where things were on the most recent day seen, worded as a time of day ("X is on Y in the morning"). "Rule" means a note that says where something is under a stated condition ("work days: desk; days off: bedroom"), usually with a fallback order of places to check.

Neither robot, in any home, ever calls the resident unwell. The reasons given are "leave or a job change", "off work", "non-work days" or "rest days".

7. **Overall.**
   - Standing notes: 23 / 25 / 26 / 28 / 28 / 29.
   - About 95% or more are snapshots of the latest day ("X is on Y in the morning"), rewritten as the sightings change. One note states a condition: claim_0001, "Weekday daytime and evening" under "when true". No note separates work days from days off.

### Verdict, hh_s2

Its faults (it dropped the first spell's dates at night 35, and kept two standing defaults that contradict each other) are small next to B's, which never notices either change and keeps "office desk ... 09:32-22:59" through both.

## Patterns across the homes (each home judged separately above)

- Whichever robot writes rules (A in hh_s2 and hh_s32, B in hh_s48) detects both spells in every home. It gets the start within a day, the end on the right day, and the second start within a day.
- Even that robot never keeps the first spell's dates in a standing note once the second spell begins. The dates survive only in set-aside notes or in a when-true line ("from day 24 on"). It never compares the second spell's length with the first, and never estimates how long the second will last.
- Whichever robot writes snapshots (B in hh_s2 and hh_s32, A in hh_s48) never records any change. It keeps a stale summary of the person standing next to object notes that contradict it.
