## Leak check: what is in these prompts that the robot's own looks could not give it

Read against every field of the six prompts below (and grepped: no "sick", "unwell", "weekend",
weekday names other than one generic example sentence, no role or occupation words, no "stage",
"calendar", "schedule" or "routine"; the 90 hits on "gym" are the `gym_bag_*` object ids in
sightings).

Given, not derived from looks:
1. The map of the house: every room and the spots in each (fixed, the same in every prompt).
2. "Two people live in it, and from the names on their things they are called Ines and Tomas":
   the count and names of the residents, resolved by the harness from the seed
   (`who_lives_here.py`) rather than read off the object ids; the object ids carry the same
   names, so the content is derivable, only the step is done for it.
3. The day index and the clock ("day 14 at 08:09"): a calendar day number and time of day, no
   weekday name.
4. The fixed instructions, including the two generic examples ("a cup when they move rooms", "a
   different room on a Saturday").

Derived from looks only: every sighting line, the "People there:" names (the people the look saw
in the room), the "not found" lists, "how long since you last looked in each room", the record
shown for the asked object, and "what you saw while looking just now".

Not present anywhere: the calendar stages (lead / sick / return), who is ill or that anyone is,
resident roles, schedules or routines, the resident intro cards, the hint messages the told arms
get, the list of objects it will be asked about ("Nobody has said which things"), the weekday.
So the arm cannot learn that an illness has started except by seeing the resident at home by day,
and cannot learn the car trips except by seeing the driver gone and then back.
