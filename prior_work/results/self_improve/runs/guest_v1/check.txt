VERDICT: DO NOT RUN. 1 check(s) failed.
  1. 'a timetable with a three-day memory' only reaches 69% by the end of the settled window, under the 70% needed - the ordinary routine is not learnable here, so nothing can be lost at the upset either

Warnings (5) - not disqualifying, but say them in the paper:
  1. 41% of moved objects do not return to where they were - the reversion measurement is mostly measuring the world, not the memory
  2. break (never forgets), re-learn (never forgets), break (three-day memory) passes with under 2 points to spare, so that leg is decided by noise rather than by the scenario; do not quote it as a margin
  3. this verdict is not robust to the thresholds: moving every bar together by 2 points reverses it at -2 (more lenient). The bars were set from what one scenario and two older ones showed, so a verdict that turns on them is the gate's opinion rather than the scenario's - do not quote it without this caveat
  4. 'a timetable that never forgets' re-learn is +5.7 points with a household-clustered error of 4.2, inside two standard errors of nothing; this null only excludes a change larger than about 14 points, it does not show there is none
  5. 'a timetable that never forgets' break again is +3.4 points with a household-clustered error of 3.7, inside two standard errors of nothing; this null only excludes a change larger than about 11 points, it does not show there is none
