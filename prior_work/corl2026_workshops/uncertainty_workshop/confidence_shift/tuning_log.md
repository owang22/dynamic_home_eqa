## Why the accepted configuration looks like it does

- Uniform-in-time questions (configs 1-24) never gave a drop: an object's last patrol sighting is right ~80% of the time on every day, because the simulator puts things back at rest spots and the weekend does not change the rest spots. Measured from truth alone (no agent): 'where it was at 03:00 today' is 78-83% right every day; 'where it was at the same time yesterday' is 69% on weekdays and 62% on Saturday. Any class mix that showed a Saturday drop on seeds 0-9 (C_t03: +15) did not survive a regenerated household sample (C_t03_sick15: -2).
- Activity-driven questions (configs 25-27) ask at the moments people need things: a few minutes around an activity start, about an object that activity uses (breakfast -> mug/bowl; cooking -> pan, knife; evening TV -> remote, blanket; a sick day's rest on the couch -> medication, blanket, laptop), plus chores the robot could do after someone has left (vacuum, laundry basket, dog bowl). This is when a home robot is actually asked, and it is when the last patrol listing is stale. Weekends change which activities happen and when (sleeping in, cooking, baking, board games), so the weekday pattern fails there: the Saturday drop is +11 to +13 on three independent household samples.
- The Wed->Fri rise (+8 accepted, -4 on another sample) is NOT learning: under a full-house patrol every classical belief in the repo reduces to the last patrol listing (most frequent and timetable disagree with last seen on 14 and 11 of 2240 answers), because every patrol lists every spot and the base pipeline's negative evidence (24 h half-life) suppresses every spot but the one the object was just seen on. The rise is the households' week, not the agents. A probe with the negative-evidence half-life cut to 0.5 h gave most frequent 61/58/58 on Wed/Thu/Fri: still no rise. Criterion (1) is therefore unmet and cannot be met by these agents on this stream; reported, not loosened.
- The event-day drop vs the day before is ~0 pooled because 'the day before' is itself noisy (32 questions) and a guest evening only touches the last third of a day's questions. Grouped by day type instead, on the accepted households: plain weekdays 60%, sick days 55%, guest days 57%, weekend 54% (last seen). Event rates were raised to sick 0.10 and guest 0.10 per weekday so that most households carry a weekday event; the generator only has weekday/weekend rates, so the event cannot be pinned to Monday.
- Patrol every 2 h: at 4 h and 8 h the whole range sat lower (47-60) with the same drop; at 1 h the drop shrank to +6-8. Two hours is a realistic round for a home robot and leaves the counters at 52-65%.
- The 'timetable' agent's bins are set to the patrol spacing (2 h) so each bin holds one pass per day; with 1 h bins it fell back to most-frequent for every question whose hour had no patrol.

Reading of the accepted protocol on the held-out set (classical): the counters LEARN the weekday routine from the
outcomes of questions (timetable +24 on moved questions Wed->Fri, most frequent +21), the weekend BREAKS it
(-6 / -12 on moved questions Fri->Sat; working households -6 / -9 pooled, retired 0), recency agents stay flat
and confident (last seen 0.98, periodic ~0.75 on moved questions where they score 15-25%). The count-based
agents hedge on moved questions (confidence ~0.4); "confident and wrong" is the recency agents' behaviour.
