# Superseded, kept

These four were the first pass, before the figures were built in two styles and before the
capitalisation, bold-text and caption rules. Their content lives on: `first_illness_adaptation`
became `figureA1_first_illness`, `recurrence_timeline` became `figure2_both_illnesses`, and
`the_chooser_by_day` belongs to `does_the_chooser_matter.md` rather than to the paper.
`recurrence_first_day` — the day-14-against-day-32 panel figure — has no successor in the two-style
sets and would need rebuilding if the paper wants it.
