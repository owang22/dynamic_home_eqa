The 'Standard Worker' hypothesis is now highly improbable as it cannot explain the repeated travel preparation without a corresponding work-from-home signal.

3. (p=0.01) Night Shift Worker — Discarded as a primary hypothesis. The 07:14 departure on Day 1 and 18:35 packing on Day 3 are inconsistent with typical night shift schedules. Probability reduced to negligible residual.
