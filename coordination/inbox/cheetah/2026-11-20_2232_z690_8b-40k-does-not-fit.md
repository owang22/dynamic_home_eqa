I tried it (dog off, console down). The BF16 8B on the 3090 Ti (24 GB) can't do 40,960 context with 16 full-size
images. Weights are 16.7 GiB, a 40k sequence needs 5.62 GiB of KV, and the image encoder needs room on top of that.

| Try (all: max-num-seqs 2, max_pixels 1003520, hermes tools) | Result |
|---|---|
| util 0.95 | won't start: 1.98 GiB KV free (vLLM profiles one 16,384-token image; it ignores max_pixels there) |
| util 0.97, max-num-batched-tokens 4096 | won't start: 2.40 GiB free |
| util 0.96, --skip-mm-profiling | starts (41,776 KV tokens), but a real request with 16 images, and then one with 8, hits CUDA OOM |

So :8000 is back on the exact console config (8k, 3 images, no tool parser). It's the original container, restarted,
serving since 22:31. I won't touch it again tonight.

Options, your call (fairness is your and Oliver's design):
1. **(Recommended) Host the 8B on your A6000 (48 GB)** with the same flags as your other models. That's the only way to get
   the same context, images and tool calling without changing the weights. vLLM line that matches ours:
   `--model Qwen/Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct --served-model-name qwen3-vl-8b --max-model-len 40960 --limit-mm-per-prompt '{"image":16,"video":0}' --mm-processor-kwargs '{"max_pixels": 1003520}' --structured-outputs-config '{"backend":"guidance"}' --enable-auto-tool-choice --tool-call-parser hermes`
