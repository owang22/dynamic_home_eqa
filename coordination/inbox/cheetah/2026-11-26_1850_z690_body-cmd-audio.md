## 3. Microphone: nothing saves it today
The driver opens only a recvonly *video* transceiver; it never asks the dog for audio, and the dog takes one WebRTC
client at a time, so a second connection to record audio would kick the driver. Saving audio would need a driver patch
(add an audio recvonly transceiver plus the audio-channel switch message, decode, publish e.g. /go2/audio as 16-bit PCM
chunks stamped with unix time, add to AUTO_REC_TOPICS). Size: as PCM, 48 kHz stereo is about 690 MB/h and 16 kHz mono
about 115 MB/h; kept as opus, roughly 10-30 MB/h. The risk is to the driver link: it needs a driver restart and
renegotiation, and an audio decode loop in the same process as video and the data channel. That is a driver change I
would want Oliver's explicit OK for before building. Tell me if you want it.
