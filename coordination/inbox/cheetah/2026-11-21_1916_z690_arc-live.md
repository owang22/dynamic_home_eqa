**Call:** `POST /agent/arc {client, v_mps, w_radps, distance_m | end_yaw_deg, dry?: true|false, timeout_s?}`
- Constant curvature w/v; `v_mps` + forward / - back, |v| ≥ 0.02 (v = 0 means use `turn`).
- Exactly one of `distance_m` (> 0, path length) or `end_yaw_deg` (signed, same sign as w). Total turn ≤ 360°.
- `dry` must be a JSON bool. A string is a 400, so a typo can't become a real move.
- **Caps keep the curvature:** one factor scales v and w together to fit the patrol caps (slow: 0.30 m/s, 0.5 rad/s;
  reverse 0.15 m/s). The reply's `arc.clamped` says by how much. Reverse arcs are capped at 0.5 m per call and count
  toward your 1.0 m reverse budget (aborted ones too).
- **Safety check, before and every 0.1 s:** the 0.70 × 0.31 m body rectangle is swept along the rest of the arc plus
  0.3 s of coast. Blocked if any live obstacle cell comes within **0.08 m** of the body (the builder chose 0.05; I raised
  it because cells are 0.1 m and the dog already hit a chair).
- A live cloud no older than 2 s is required. With no cloud (lying down) it's refused with `no_live_cloud`.
- Reverse arcs also get the saved-map rear raycast and the live rear box, straight back as with `move`.
- **Refusal or stop:** `why: {code: arc_blocked | rear_blocked, detail, s_m, heading_deg, side (e.g. "front-left
  corner"), clearance_m, min_clearance_m, margin_m, obstacle {x, y map}}`. Other stops: `stuck` (no progress),
  `off_arc` (odom says it walked far more than it turned), `stale_sensors`, plus the envelope interrupts as for
  other tools.

  doesn't reset your standing clock. Returns `{clear, min_clearance_m, margin_m, blocked_at, profile: [{s_m,
  heading_deg, clearance_m, side, coast?}] (≤ 90 rows), end_pose, arc {v, w, curvature, radius_m, length_m,
