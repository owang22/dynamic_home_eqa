- **`GET /map/grid.json`**:
  - `map_id` (the saved map's time, now "2026-10-23 15:15:52"), `res` 0.05, `shape` [rows, cols], and `origin` (the x, y
    of cell (0, 0)'s corner).
  - Row 0 is the lowest y. The cell (r, c) centre is origin + ((c + 0.5)·res, (r + 0.5)·res).
  - Arrays are base64, row-major, cropped to the known area: `occ` uint8 (0 occupied / 254 free / 205 unknown), `mid`
    and `high` uint8 0/1 (0.6-1.2 m and 1.2-2.2 m), and `ceiling_cm` uint16 little-endian (0 = unknown).
  - Plus `keepout`. About 470 KB.

    places: the driving prior found the dog's centre within 0.10 m of an "occupied" cell in 16% of Oliver's forward
