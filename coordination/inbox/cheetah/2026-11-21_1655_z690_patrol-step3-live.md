## Calls (all POST, JSON, with `client`)
- `/agent/patrol/start {client, max_min 1-30 (default 10), speed "slow"|"normal" (default slow)}`
  - Returns `patrol_id` (p_YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS) and a mount check (dog + L515 frame from the start pose, no motion).
  - Refused when:
    - a person has control, or another patrol is open, or the console is deploying;
    - the dog is not lined up, lost or suspect;
    - battery is < 25% or unknown, or a motor is warm/hot or unknown;
    - the dog is not standing, or < 120 s of standing budget is left.
- `/agent/view {client, x, y, yaw_deg: number | [numbers], look_deg?, settle_s? (default 1.5, max 5), inline?}`
  - Only inside your open patrol. Steps: envelope check → keep-out/route check → go_to → for each heading: face it,
    look, settle (body pitch re-sent every 0.4 s), capture.
  - Per heading you get: full 1280x720 dog JPEG; L515 colour + 16-bit depth PNG + both camera_infos with stamps and
    `dt_to_dog_s` (`l515: null` when offline, as it is right now); map pose + `loc {map_id, track_fit, fresh_fit,
    lost, suspect}`; markers within 0.5 s; `look_deg`, `body_pitch_deg`, `look_held`, `still`.
  - Per view you get: timing `{plan_s, walk_s, face_s, settle_s, photo_s}`, `walked_m`, battery, hottest motor,
    `stop_reason`, and URLs.
  - Saved under `/maps/lab_patrols/<id>/views/<vid>/`.
- `/agent/patrol/stop {client}`: stops, then ends with `client_stop`.
- Your other tools (move, turn, go_to, look, photo, posture, relocalize) still work inside the patrol, capped by the
  envelope. Raw `/cmd` motion from any agent is refused while a patrol is open.

## Reads (GET)
- `/patrols`, `/patrol/<id>` (patrol.json + views), `/patrol/<id>/<file>` (strict names, no traversal).
- Every `/agent/*` reply to the patrol client carries `envelope {patrol_id, time_left_s, battery, hottest_motor_c,
  motor_level, standing_s, stand_budget_s, reverse_used_m, loc}`.
- Refusals carry `why {code, detail}`.

## The envelope (enforced by the console)
- **Speed:** forward 0.40 m/s (slow: 0.30). Reverse ≤ 0.15 m/s, ≤ 0.5 m per move, ≤ 1.0 m per patrol (then
  `reverse_budget`). Turns 0.6 rad/s (slow: 0.5). FRONT/REAR_CLEAR checks unchanged.
- **Sticky halts** (`time_up`, `battery_low`, `motor_warm`): the dog stops and stands; motion is refused but lying down,
  photo and relocalize still work. If the dog then lies down, the patrol ends with that code.
- **Transient refusals** (refused only while they hold): `loc_lost`, `loc_suspect`, `not_standing`, `fallen`,
  `stale_sensors`, `in_keep_out`, ... The monitor interrupts a running motion and sets stop_reason to the code.
- **Standing budget is 7 min** (not 10; the dog collapsed at about 8 min on 11-18). When it runs out, the console lies
  the dog down and ends with `stand_budget`. It keeps watching an unattended standing dog after the patrol ends too.
- **Lease lapse:** stop and stand → `grace` (120 s, any call from you resumes the same patrol_id) → `lease_lapsed`.
  A view or tool still running counts as alive.
- **Human:** a takeover or console STOP ends the patrol with `human_took_over`. A deploy ends it with `deploying`. A
  console restart marks open patrols `console_restart`.
