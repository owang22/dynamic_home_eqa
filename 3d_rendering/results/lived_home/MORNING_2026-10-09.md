  with...", and the sick resident's line only said "this is happening to you". Mara (A) kept
  health 3.0 on her first sick day and Daniel (B) never reported illness; in B both acted as
  carers. Now: "where this speaks of one of you, that is you". A's days 8-14 were re-run with
  the fix (health 1.2 on days 8-10, home all day, recovering by day 12). **B's sick days 5-7
  are not usable as a sick spell.**
- **Gaps step typed things "object"** (B's first creation: 11 trinkets typed "object", no forks
  added). The reason now comes before the type, and generic types are refused. B recreated.
- **Network faults stopped A twice** (a dropped connection, then a Cloudflare 520). The client now
  retries both; A resumed from its checkpoints (resumes.json records the commits).
- **Questions** (from the two blind play-tests): put-away questions only about things the patrols
  have seen; "put away" judged by the piece of furniture (a robot cannot tell its drawers apart; a
  bag lying on it counts); look-alikes are asked for by owner and unowned look-alikes not asked;
  counts dropped from descriptions ("two yellow banana"); singular "thermos"; fewer questions about
  things that never move; rules explain look-alikes, bags and worn things; the CLI checks ids.
- Lounge pants go with sleepwear; empty remarks refused; a washer is known from the scene.
