   later rule changes do not shift these numbers). A thing's usual place is where it started,
   except on spots people drop things (floors, beds, seats, basins, stovetops, washer drums,
   laundry baskets); shoes and large things live on floors, bedding on beds, soap and shampoo in

   owner's commonest drawer or wardrobe. A: 20 of 251 things have none, B: 15 of 272.

   in X)", at most 8 per view. Not for dirty clothes, nor for clothes worn today. Things that moved
   to another shelf or table are left alone (they may have found a new place). Nothing tells
   residents to put things back. Logged per call (`kept_shown`); `--no_kept_cue` turns it off; a

## Decisions for you

   creation prompts ask for things "where it would be right now in the early morning" and for
   "clothes they leave lying around", so a start spot is often where a thing is in use. My rules
   recover most of it (spot-checked by hand on both households), but a "kept at" field asked for
   at creation would be cleaner. Only matters for new households (paid).

   twice at one commit, notes on and off (about $1.7 together). Measure lying-about by day, and how

   note is an order). $3.17 of the $16 cap is left. Or free on the local Qwen once the GPU is back
   (nvidia-smi still cannot reach the driver).
