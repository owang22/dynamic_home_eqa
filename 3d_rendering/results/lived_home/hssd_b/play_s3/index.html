The home, seen from above

Point at a box to see what it is. Click it to look (or to place a guess, when guessing).
How this works
  • You are the robot. You know the floor plan and who owns what. You do not know where things are, except from the patrol notes: at 08:00, 12:00, 16:00 and 20:00 each day the robot walked through a few rooms (not all) and noted what it could see.
  • The robot cannot open drawers, cupboards, wardrobes or the fridge, does not see under things, and cannot see inside closed bags or boxes.
  • If you think a thing is out of sight, answer Put away in... and pick the piece of furniture (a drawer of it, or a bag or box lying on it: both count as put away there). Has it (at home) means a resident is holding, carrying or wearing it at home (a look shows what people in the room hold, not what they wear; what someone holds while standing at a piece of furniture also shows in a look at that furniture, and you can click it there); Out of the house means it went out with them.
  • Some things look alike (two identical phones, two clear steel water bottles): the notes cannot tell them apart, so a sighting may be either one. A question about one of them names its owner.
  • Each question is at a time between patrols. First, if you like, set up to three ranked guesses and a note on each (click Place guess, then a box on the plan).
  • Then look: click a box to walk there and see what is on it right now. You have up to looks; a look that shows nothing means the surface really is empty. When you see the thing, click it in the list to say "that's it". If you think it is out of sight, answer without seeing it.
  • Everything you see in your looks is kept and shows up under Your earlier looks for later questions, so a look can be worth it even when it does not find the thing.
  • After each answer you see where it really was. Rate how clear the question was and how answerable, and write anything that seems off.
