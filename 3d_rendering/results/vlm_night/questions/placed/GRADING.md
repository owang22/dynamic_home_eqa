Proposed grading (from the survey; no benchmark gives credit for naming a neighbouring piece of furniture):
- Report side by side: rests there (support, from the household's own place record: on top / inside /
  floor), shows in the look (>= T px in that look's pictures, at 30 px and 0.1% of the frame = 307 px),
  and the room. Plus the count of searches that stopped at a neighbour.
- Headline: rests there. It does not depend on where cameras stand and matches answer-the-question
  benchmarks (OVMM placing, PARTNR, HD-EPIC). Use "shows in the look" when the claim is about searching.
- "Put away", "someone has it", "out of the house": right only when the state matches; naming furniture for
  a thing inside a closed drawer is never "found"; a held thing seen in a look counts for "shows in the look".
- Report rank 1 and within 8, next to the no-memory and most-often rules.
