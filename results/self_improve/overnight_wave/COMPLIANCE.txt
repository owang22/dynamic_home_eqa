  WHAT THE ZERO MEANS, and what it does not. `contradicting_observation_ids` is
  the field a READER of the notes sees: at answer time a note with four sightings
  for and none against reads as a well-supported belief. Zero there does NOT mean
  the arms never look for disconfirmation - the 'said so and rewrote' column is
  the model identifying a refutation and acting on it correctly. The gap is
  between noticing and RECORDING, and it is the recording that a later night, or
  a reader, or an answer step can use.
  'unchanged' is an UPPER BOUND and the whole column is a regex over prose: some
  of those notes were dealt with at a later edit than the one carrying the phrase.
  'was a negation' is the regex's own false-positive rate, counted rather than
  estimated: 'No sightings today contradicted this' matches the word and means the
  opposite. Those are excluded from every other column here. An earlier version of
  this measure did not exclude them and reported a third of notes as carrying an
  invisible refutation.
