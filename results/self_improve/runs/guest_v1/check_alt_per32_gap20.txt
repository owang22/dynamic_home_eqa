ALTERNATIVE BUILD of guest_v1, saved 2026-09-24 for the record - NOT the committed run.
Identical households, event rules and object classes; the only differences are
per_day: 32 (instead of 24) and question_min_gap_min: 20 (instead of 30) - i.e. the robot
is asked more often, which gives a forgetting learner more feedback to learn the ordinary
fortnight from. It lifts the settled floor from 69.2% to 72.9% and clears it, and the break
and the return get better too; but the never-forgetting learner's re-learning leg then falls
to +4.6 against a +5.0 bar, because with more data it has already converged by the end of the
settled window and has little left to gain during the disruption. Six of seven either way.
It is NOT the committed build because per_day 24 / gap 30 is what illness_v1, room_shut_v1,
wfh_v1 and night_shift_v1 all use, and changing it for one scenario would make the
cross-scenario table not like for like.
