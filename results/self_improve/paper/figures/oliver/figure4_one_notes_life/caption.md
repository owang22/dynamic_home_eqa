# Figure 4. One note's life.

The same note in the claim store - claim_0005 of household hh_s2_t03, the untold arm - as it
stood on four nights: before the illness, four days into it, on the day ordinary life returns, and
a week after. Quotes and the condition line are verbatim. The text a note carried on night N is
read as the text its next revision replaced, which is how the store records history.

## The claim this figure is here to make

A memory that could recognise a returning situation would end the month holding two readings
of this glass, one for ordinary days and one for the days its owner is unwell, and a condition
saying which is which. This one holds a single reading and overwrites it each time. On night 18 the
bedroom nightstand is simply where the glass is; by night 31 the bedroom is gone and the condition
field reads "Always" - the most general condition the format allows, on a placement that was true
for ten days of the month. Nothing here was lost through forgetting: the note was rewritten every
time, and each rewrite was correct about the day it was written on.
