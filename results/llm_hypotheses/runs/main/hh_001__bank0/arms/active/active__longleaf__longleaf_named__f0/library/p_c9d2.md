# p_c9d2 — Water Bottle at the Nightstand Only Before 6:00 (fork of p_5d8b)

This fork of p_5d8b addresses the water bottle's early-morning location. The sibling document p_7e4a placed the bottle at the nightstand from 6:00 to 8:30, but that claim has accumulated 10 against sightings (only 2 for). However, the "MIXTURE'S WORST OBJECTS" log shows the water bottle at nightstand_b1 at 05:00 on day 10 (twice), which is BEFORE the 6:00 start of p_7e4a's window. The interpretation: Mara keeps the water bottle on the nightstand while in bed (roughly 4:00–6:00), then moves it to the counter or takes it with her when she gets up. By 6:00 the bottle has already left the nightstand, which explains why the 6:00–8:30 window fails so badly.

What changed from p_5d8b: (1) water_bottle_mara gains a "both 4–6h at nightstand_b1" block (early-morning bedroom period), placed after the counter default so it overrides during those hours. (2) A new claim tests the nightstand location in the 4:00–6:00 window. (3) The OUT_OF_HOUSE weekday block is retained. (4) The evening kitchen-table block is retained.

What sets this apart from p_5d8b: at 5:00 on any day, the water bottle is at the nightstand (not the counter). What sets this apart from p_7e4a: the nightstand window is 4–6h (not 6–8.5h); by 6:00 the bottle has moved.
