# p_8f2d — Plates Live at Table and Counter, Not the Cupboard (fork of p_7c3e)

This fork of p_7c3e removes the class:plate cupboard_k1 0–24h block that has been failing at 0.18 on 13 sightings across every single document in the library. The per-object data is unambiguous: plate_shared_1 is at kitchen_table_k1 on 7/13 sighted days, plate_shared_2 is at counter_k1 on 6/13 sighted days. Neither plate lives in the cupboard. The cupboard block is a legacy error inherited from the p_5d8b lineage and has never held up. Since both plates have individual target entries that fully specify their locations, the class:plate block is redundant and actively harmful (it pulls the prediction toward the cupboard for any plate sighting not covered by the individual entries).

What changed from p_7c3e: (1) Removed the class:plate entry entirely from targets. The individual plate_shared_1 and plate_shared_2 entries remain and fully cover both known plates. (2) All other blocks, claims, and the narrowed 18–21h evening chair window are unchanged.

What sets this apart from p_7c3e: if the robot encounters a plate at 10:00, this document does not predict the cupboard; it falls through to the robot's own statistics. What would refute it: a plate sighted in the cupboard_k1 on multiple occasions, or the tablet at the coffee table during weekday 9–14.
