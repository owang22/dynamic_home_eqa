# p_f6b3 — Phone Stays Home on Some Workdays; Nightstand is the Default Resting Place

This is a fresh document addressing a pattern the library handles too rigidly. Every existing document places phone_mara at OUT_OF_HOUSE during weekday 8.5–14.5 with "usually" confidence. However, the p_8e1b claim that the phone is out of the house during that window has 3 against sightings (the phone was found in the house during 9–14h on three separate occasions) and only 3 weak-for. The per-object statistics show phone_mara at 5 distinct receptacles with nightstand_b1 holding only 6/10 sighted days, and weekday 9–17h looks at nightstand_b1 found it 3 times versus 6 empty. The 3 in-house finds during work hours mean the phone does NOT always travel.

Interpretation: Mara takes her phone to the co-working space on most weekdays, but on some days (perhaps remote-work days, or days she works from a nearby café and keeps her phone in the house charging) the phone stays at the nightstand or moves to the kitchen table. The nightstand is the default resting place when the phone is home. The OUT_OF_HOUSE block should be "sometimes" rather than "usually" to reflect this variability.

What sets this apart from p_8e1b and all its forks: the phone is at the nightstand on some weekday mornings (not always out of the house). The OUT_OF_HOUSE chance is "sometimes" not "usually." What would refute it: the phone found at the nightstand on every single weekday morning (which would mean it never travels), or the phone never found in the house during 9–14h (which would mean it always travels and the 3 against sightings were anomalies).
