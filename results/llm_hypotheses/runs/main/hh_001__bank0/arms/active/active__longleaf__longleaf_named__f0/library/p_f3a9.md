# p_f3a9 — Tablet Travels on Some Work Days; Counter on Others (fork of p_5d8b)

This fork of p_5d8b offers a hybrid account of the tablet's weekday work-hour behaviour. The evidence is inconsistent: the tablet is found at counter_k1 on 3 of 9 weekday 9–17h looks (supporting "stays home at the counter"), but on 6 of those looks it is absent from the counter (supporting "out of the house" or "at the other receptacle"). The p_3a7f document (tablet always out) has 3 against sightings (tablet found in-house during 9–14), so it is not always out. The p_9c2e document (tablet at kitchen table) has 0 for, 6 against, so the kitchen table is wrong. The p_5d8b document (tablet at coffee table) keeps accumulating against sightings.

The interpretation: on most work days the tablet stays home at the counter (Mara uses it for reference, or it is simply where it was left). On 1–2 days per week the tablet goes with Mara to the co-working space (perhaps on days she needs a second screen, or on days the co-working space lacks a monitor). This is a "sometimes travels" pattern, analogous to the water bottle and lunchbox.

What changed from p_5d8b: (1) tablet_mara's default is counter_k1 (not coffee_table_l1). (2) A weekday 8.5–14.5 OUT_OF_HOUSE block with "sometimes" chance is added (the tablet travels on some work days). (3) The evening couch block is retained. (4) The claim tests the tablet at counter_k1 during weekday 9–14 (on the days it stays home).

What sets this apart from p_a4f7 (the sibling): p_a4f7 says the tablet is ALWAYS at the counter during work hours; this document says it is at the counter on most days but OUT on some. What sets this apart from p_3a7f: the tablet is NOT always out; it is in the house on most work days. What would refute it: the tablet found at the coffee table or kitchen table during weekday 9–14, or the tablet found in the house on 4+ consecutive work days during 9–14 (which would contradict the "sometimes out" block).
