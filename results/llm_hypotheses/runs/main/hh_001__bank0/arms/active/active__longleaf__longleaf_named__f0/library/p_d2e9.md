# p_d2e9 — Plates at Table and Counter Only; No Cupboard Block (fork of p_8f2d)

This fork fixes a bug in p_8f2d: the parent's prose states "Removed the class:plate entry entirely from targets," but the JSON block still contains the class:plate at cupboard_k1 0–24h block. The class:plate cupboard prediction has failed at 0.12 on 21 sightings across every document in the library. The per-object data is clear: plate_shared_1 is at kitchen_table_k1 on 11/20 sighted days, plate_shared_2 is at counter_k1 on 9/20 sighted days. Neither plate lives in the cupboard. The individual plate_shared_1 and plate_shared_2 entries fully specify their locations, making the class:plate block redundant and actively harmful.

What changed from p_8f2d: (1) The class:plate entry is actually removed from the targets JSON. (2) All other blocks, claims, and the narrowed 18–21h evening chair window are identical to p_8f2d.

What sets this apart from p_8f2d: when the robot encounters a plate at any hour not covered by the individual plate entries, this document falls through to the robot's own statistics rather than predicting the cupboard. What would refute it: a plate sighted in the cupboard_k1 on multiple occasions, or the tablet at the coffee table during weekday 9–14.
