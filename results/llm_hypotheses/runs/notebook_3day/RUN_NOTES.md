## What happened

- 523 calls (511 live), 85 min wall; 5 forks (2 at follow-ups on d00,
  3 forced by the end-of-day reviews), 0 retirements, population 4 -> 9.
- Only 3 looks in 3 days, all on d00 between 06:02 and 06:14. After the
  third look a04 held 0.99 of the weight: a look scores every one of the
  35 objects, so a 3-4 nat gap between agents on one look is a 20-50x
  weight ratio. With one agent at 0.99 both Thompson draws pick it in
  98% of rounds, so the disagreement trigger stopped firing and the
  budget went unspent on d01 and d02 (`budget_exhausted_at` never).
  This is the design as specified (no tempering; overreactions visible)
  and is the first thing to judge from this run.
- The end-of-day review picked a02 (weight ~1e-6) every day; its forks
  inherit half of nothing and stay at ~1e-6, so the review kept forking
  the same lineage (a05, a07, a08, a09 are all a02's) without changing
  the mixture.
- Accuracy (exact, queried objects): d00 0.50, d01 0.38, d02 0.67; 0.51
  over 72 questions.
- 19 of 491 question forecasts came back as `"spots": []` (the why named
  an answer the list left out); the floor makes such a forecast uniform.
  Fixed after the run by `minItems: 1` on the spots list (schema-level;
  the run above still carries them).
- Prompts, notebooks and forks read cleanly: the four cold-start
  documents are genuinely contrasting (rigid routine / chaotic drifter /
  zone storage / activity-driven), follow-ups write dated scratch
  entries and reasoned forks, no truncation, no cap violations.
