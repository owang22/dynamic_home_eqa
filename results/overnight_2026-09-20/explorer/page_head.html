Patrol run explorer

situation_sim, 8-day episodes (Tuesday walkthrough, Wednesday to Tuesday scored), robot patrol every 4 h, 32 questions a day. Every agent on the same bank; classical beliefs with the look off and with the one-step VoI look; LLM agents (Qwen3.8-27B, no thinking) with three memories × told / not told × look on / off. Nothing here is a reasoning trace: the LLM answers a fixed JSON schema; the routine-summary agent's daily notes are the only text it writes.

Each dot is one household: its own shift-day accuracy minus its own non-shift-day accuracy, for that agent. The bar is the mean over households.
