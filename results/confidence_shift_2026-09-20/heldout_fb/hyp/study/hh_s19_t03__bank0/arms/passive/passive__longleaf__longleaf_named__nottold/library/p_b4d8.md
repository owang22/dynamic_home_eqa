# p_b4d8 — The 18:30 cook: pan emerges at half past, snacks stay on the counter, remote never moves

This document synthesises the two patterns the library keeps getting wrong separately. The cooking window is NOT 17:50–19:00 (as p_a1b2 and p_789a assume) but 18:30–19:30. The 18:00 patrol shows the pan still in the cupboard, the knife still in the drawer, and the pot still in the cupboard. The 19:00 patrol shows the pan and knife on the counter. The 20:00 patrol shows the pan back in the cupboard. Cooking is a tight one-hour window centred on 19:00.

Simultaneously, the snack bowl does NOT go to the coffee table. It appears on counter_k1 at 18:00 (2×) and at 20:00 (3×). The residents grab snacks from the kitchen counter while watching TV, not from the living room. And the remote stays at tv_stand_l1 throughout the evening; the p_789a claim that it moves to the coffee table has scored 0-for / 3-against.

What sets this apart: the pan is in the cupboard at 18:00 (not on the counter), the snack bowl is on the kitchen counter during TV (not the coffee table), and the remote is at the tv stand during TV (not the coffee table). All three of these positions are simultaneously in effect from 19:30 to 22:00.

What would refute it: pan_shared sighted on counter_k1 at 18:00; or snack_bowl_shared at coffee_table_l1 at 20:00; or remote_shared at coffee_table_l1 at 20:00.
