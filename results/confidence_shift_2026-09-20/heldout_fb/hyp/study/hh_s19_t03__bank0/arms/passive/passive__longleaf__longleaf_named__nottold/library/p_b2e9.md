# p_b2e9 — Late dinner at 19:00: the living room comes first, the pan never leaves the cupboard

A different evening rhythm than the library's current cooking-together models. After Marco gets home around 17:30, they do **not** go straight to the kitchen. Instead they settle into the living room for a relaxed 90-minute block (17:30–19:00): Omar is still winding down from his desk, Marco is decompressing from the office. The remote is on the coffee table, the blanket is on the couch, maybe a magazine. They are watching something or just talking.

Cooking is a **one-person, later** task. Around 19:00, one of them (usually Marco) gets up and heads to the kitchen to make dinner while the other stays on the couch. The cooking vessel is the **pot**, not the pan. The pan is a specialty item (stir-fry, searing) that gets used maybe once a week; for the regular pasta, soup, or rice-and-vegetables dinner, the pot goes on the counter and the pan stays in the cupboard. This is consistent with the 8 empty looks at counter_k1 for the pan during 17:50–19:00 and the single sighting of the pan in cupboard_k1.

Dinner is at the kitchen table 19:30–20:00. Then the second TV block: 20:00–23:00, both on the couch, snack bowl out, mugs on the coffee table. Bedtime 23:30.

What sets this apart from the other documents: (1) the remote is on the coffee table **before** cooking (17:30–19:00), not just after; (2) the pan is in the cupboard for the entire 17:00–20:00 window on most days; (3) cooking is 19:00–19:30, not 17:30–19:00; (4) the two residents are in **different rooms** from 17:30 to 19:30 (one in living, one in kitchen after 19:00).

What would refute it: the pan sighted on counter_k1 between 17:00 and 20:00; both residents in the kitchen at the same time before 19:00; the remote still on tv_stand_l1 at 18:00 (they would already be watching).
