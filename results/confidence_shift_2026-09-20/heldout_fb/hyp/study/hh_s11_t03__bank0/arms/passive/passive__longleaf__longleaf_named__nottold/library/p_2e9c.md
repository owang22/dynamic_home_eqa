Hana's glass is scattered across the kitchen (pantry, sink, cupboard) and occasionally the coffee table during this window, reflecting her restless post-shift movements. On day 3, Hana is already in bedroom_1 at 03:00, suggesting she skipped the kitchen snack that night.
