# p_f3a9 — Yuki's morning walk is the dog's walk: leash on person, food bag at counter

This document focuses on the tightly-coupled morning routine that Yuki runs with the household dog. On weekdays, Yuki wakes at 06:30, fills the dog's bowl from the food bag at 06:45 (food bag moves from pantry_shelf_k1 to counter_k1 briefly), and then walks the dog from 07:00 to 08:00. During the walk, the dog_leash_shared is ON_PERSON with resident_2 (Yuki). The dog returns, Yuki puts the leash back at entry_table_e1, and the food bag goes back to the pantry. On weekends, the walk is later (10:00–11:00) and the feeding is at 09:30.

What sets this apart from the other documents: during the walk window (07:00–08:00 weekdays, 10:00–11:00 weekends), the dog_leash_shared is ON_PERSON (carried by Yuki), not at entry_table_e1. The dog_food_bag_shared is at counter_k1 during the feeding window (06:45–07:15 weekdays, 09:30–10:00 weekends), not at pantry_shelf_k1. The dog_bowl_shared is at floor_k_k1 throughout (it stays put; Yuki fills it in place).

What would refute it: the dog_leash sighted at entry_table_e1 at 07:30 on a weekday (it should be ON_PERSON); the dog_food_bag at pantry_shelf_k1 at 07:00 on a weekday (it should be at the counter during feeding); the dog_bowl not at floor_k_k1 at 07:15.
