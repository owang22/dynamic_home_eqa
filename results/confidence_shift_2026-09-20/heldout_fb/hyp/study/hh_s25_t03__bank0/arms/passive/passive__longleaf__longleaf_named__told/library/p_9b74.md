# p_9b74 — p_3k7m — Desk objects visible only at the edges of work; invisible during active hours

Marco and Omar both work from home at their respective desks (desk_b1 and desk_o1) from roughly 9:00 to 17:00 on weekdays. The critical observation from seven days of sightings is that desk objects—laptop, charger, notebook, pen, mouse, headphones—are physically at the desk but **not visible** to the robot during the active work window. The robot's looks at desk_b1 during 9–17 h find the laptop only 8 times out of 38, the charger only 1 out of 38, the notebook 6 out of 38, and the pen 8 out of 38. The objects are there (7/7 sighted days at the desk), but the resident sitting in front of them blocks the robot's line of sight. They become visible again in the early morning before the work session begins and in the late afternoon after it ends.

This hypothesis therefore makes **no claims** about desk objects during the 9:30–16:30 core work window. Instead it claims them at the desk during the visible morning (7–9 h) and the visible evening (17–19 h), when the resident is not yet seated or has stood up. The charger, in particular, is at desk_b1 at 03:00, 09:00, 17:00, 18:00, 21:00, and 23:00 in the patrol data, confirming it rests at the desk outside the work window. During work it is either plugged into the laptop behind the monitor or in the resident's pocket; either way the robot cannot see it, and the 28 empty looks at desk_b1 during 10–16 h (which score as "weak for" on ON_PERSON blocks in other documents) are equally consistent with "at the desk but hidden."

Nothing in this household leaves the house on a regular schedule. Both residents work from home; the keys, wallets, and jackets stay at the entry. The water bottle is the one object that cycles through the kitchen (see p_8r2n). This document focuses on the desk-visibility pattern and the safe resting spots.

What would refute this document: if the robot consistently finds the laptop or charger at desk_b1 during the 10–15 h window on multiple days, the "invisible during work" explanation fails and the objects are simply at the desk and visible. Conversely, if the laptop is found at the coffee table or on the floor during 9–17 h on multiple days, the "at the desk but hidden" model is wrong.
