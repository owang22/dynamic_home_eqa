# p_789a — Hana's Late-Night Kitchen; Cooking at Midnight

Hana does not eat a proper dinner at home. When she comes home at 23:00, she cooks a full dinner for herself (and sometimes for Priya if she is still up) from 23:15 to 00:15. The kitchen is active at midnight: pans on the stove, knife on the counter, plates at the dining table. Priya eats an earlier, lighter dinner at 18:00–18:30. In the morning, Hana just has toast and coffee (toaster and kettle at 6:30–7:00). She works from home 7:00–13:00 at her desk. Priya's routine: dog walk 7:00–8:00, breakfast 7:30, afternoon errands 14:00–16:00, evening reading or TV 20:00–22:00, bed 22:30. On weekends, Hana sleeps until 8, cooks brunch 9:00–10:00, errands 11:00–14:00, guitar 16:00–18:00. Priya walks 10:00–12:00, cooks lunch 12:30.

What sets this apart: the kitchen is active 23:15–00:15 on weekdays (pan, knife, plates out). This is the only hypothesis where cooking happens after midnight. The toaster is used at 6:30 (not 7:00).

What would refute it: pan_shared found at cupboard_k1 at 23:30 on a weekday; kitchen_knife_shared found at drawer_k_k1 at 23:45 on a weekday; plate_hana found at sink_k1 at 23:30 on a weekday.
