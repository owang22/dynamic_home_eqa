# p_8c4e — The Desk and the Rack: Corrected Resting Places (fork of p_7e4a)

First, Hana's laptop, charger, and pen do **not** rest at entry_hook_e1 overnight. The charger is at desk_b1 three of four passes from 00:00 to 08:00 every weekday, and the laptop and pen split evenly between desk_b1 and entry_hook_e1 at night — but at 08:00 (just before she leaves) all three are at desk_b1. The entry_hook_e1 sightings at night are the sloppy "I'll put it properly in the morning" variant. The primary resting place is desk_b1. Second, Hana's water bottle does not live at entry_hook_e1 either; it rests at dish_rack_k1 or sink_k1 in the kitchen (00:00–08:00 passes show sink_k1 x3, dish_rack_k1 x1), and it appears at kitchen_table_k1 at 20:00 (x4) during the evening meal. The "blocks that failed" list confirms water_bottle_hana at entry_hook_e1 failed in four separate documents.

Third, the guitar is **never** ON_PERSON. It sits at bedroom_floor_b1 at every single clock-hour pass, all seven days, 71 sightings, one receptacle. The 19:00–20:30 ON_PERSON block in the parent has produced zero positive sightings and multiple "against" tallies. I have removed it entirely.

What sets this apart from p_7e4a: at 02:00 on a weekday, charger_hana is at desk_b1 (not entry_hook_e1), water_bottle_hana is at dish_rack_k1 (not entry_hook_e1), and guitar_hana is at bedroom_floor_b1 (not ON_PERSON). What would refute it: charger_hana sighted at entry_hook_e1 at 02:00 on a weekday, or the guitar sighted ON_PERSON at any hour.
