# What F8 does and does not support

## The claim

> Scored +1 for a right answer, −1 for a wrong one and 0 for declining, the timetables collapse at the shift while Perpetua* and long-context do not.

## What in the figure demonstrates it

The first dotted rule. The never-forgets timetable falls from 7.7 in the settled week to 0.7 on the first sick days and the 3-day timetable from 7.6 to 2.9, while Perpetua* goes 4.6 to 4.6 and long-context 4.6 to 5.1. Note also that Perpetua* sits BELOW both timetables while the world is stable.

## What it does NOT show

The thresholds are chosen with hindsight for the window being scored, so these are upper bounds, not a policy anyone could run. Each method uses a different threshold, so the lines are not a like-for-like confidence comparison — that is deliberate, since the confidence scales differ wildly, but it means a reader cannot infer anything about the thresholds themselves from this figure.
