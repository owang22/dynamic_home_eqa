# What F3 does and does not support

## The claim

> One sentence buys back much of the break and costs on the return; each told arm is identical to the untold run until the day it is told.

## What in the figure demonstrates it

Where each line begins. The told-once line emerges at day 14 and runs above the untold line for the rest of the spell — that gap is what one sentence buys. The told-twice line emerges before the return day, which is a genuine early difference in the runs and not a drawing choice; the departure days are in the numbers.

## What it does NOT show

At this household count almost nothing here clears our claim bar, and the bands overlap heavily — treat the gaps as indicative, not established. It also does not show a cost of the message on the way out for this memory; the buffer and retrieval show that more clearly and on more households.
