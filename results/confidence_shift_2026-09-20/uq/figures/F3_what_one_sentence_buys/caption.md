One long-context memory, told three different things, on the same households. The darkest line is never told; the middle line is told "Yuki is home sick today" on the first sick day; the lightest is told again that the resident is back on the first day of the return. Each told arm is the same run as the one above it until the day it is told, so it is drawn only from the day it measurably departs.
